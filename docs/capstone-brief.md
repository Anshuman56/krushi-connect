## Users

- Farmer example: “Bhanu, 45, grows tomatoes and brinjal in Pipili. Has a smartphone (Jio). Can read Odia easily, English with difficulty. Sells 50-200 kg per week.”
- Buyer example: “A restaurant owner near Infosys Bhubaneswar, buys 20kg vegetables three times a week, currently sources from Unit-1 market.”

## The ONE flagship AI feature

- crop disease detection from photo + treatment advice in Odia.
- Photo of the affected pest crop.
- I will return the disease name and what pesticide did it need to solve the pest.
- Why is feature because people are don't know about many disease and there cure.

## v1 core features (3 only)

- Farmer can list produce (crop, quantity, price, location, photo)
- Buyer can browse listings and filter by crop + location
- Buyer can contact farmer directly (WhatsApp link, no in-app chat for v1)
