### what a listing must contain?

- Farmer name: text - Buyer knows who they’re dealing with
- Crop: one of a short list (tomato, brinjal, ...) - So buyers can filter later
- Quantity: number, in kg - The restaurant needs 20kg, 3×/week
- Price: per kg number, - in ₹ Can be “negotiable” later; skip for now
- Village / location: text - Pipili vs Angul matters (Week 18 lesson)
- WhatsApp number: text, 10 digits - This is the contact feature
- Created date: set automatically - Buyers want fresh listings
